# Steps

1. Book the service for the same month each year, before the heating season starts.
2. Confirm the engineer is Gas Safe registered (or local equivalent) before the visit.
3. Keep the service certificate with the household's other maintenance records.
4. Note any parts replaced or issues flagged for next year's visit.

# Why annually

See the claim that an
[annual boiler service prevents most emergency callouts](../claims/annual-boiler-service-prevents-most-callouts.md),
which is the evidence behind keeping this on a fixed yearly schedule rather
than waiting for a fault.
