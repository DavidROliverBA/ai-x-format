# Steps

1. Set a reminder for six weeks before the renewal date.
2. Get at least two comparison quotes covering the same cover level.
3. Compare against the incumbent insurer's quoted renewal price, not last year's price.
4. Switch if a comparison quote beats the renewal price by a meaningful margin; otherwise call the incumbent and ask them to match it.

# Why bother comparing

See the claim that
[getting renewal quotes beats accepting auto-renewal](../claims/insurance-renewal-quotes-beat-auto-renew.md),
which is the evidence behind never letting this auto-renew unchecked.
